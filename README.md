# Temporal Industries

Temporal Industries is a tech mod about controlling time. Record a point in time and jump back to it whenever you want - whether it is to rebuild a part of your house that didnt turn out as you wanted, something exploded, or you just want a change of scenery. Glue parts of your base with Temporal glue to make it immune to time travel (handy for chests!)

***

## Timelines

Chrono- prefixed machines snapshot a part of your world into **commits** that are bound to a named **repository**. You can change a machine to a different repository at any time. Select a commit and press jump to roll the area back to the selected commit. Jumps have an energy cost that depends on how much has to change.

***

## Time loops

**Echo Record**

*   Captures your movement along with your interactions: breaking and placing blocks, using items, working with containers etc.

**Echo Projector**

*   Lets you insert an Echo Record. It summons an Echo that loops the recording forever. It appears as a translucent ghost of you. You can dye the projector to recolor its echoes! :3

## Entropy

Manipulating time isn't free. Entropy is a new resource you have to manage. Its comprised of two new fluids - Chaos and Order, with some interesting ways to generate them. Some machines consume entropy, others need you to reach a precise balance.
